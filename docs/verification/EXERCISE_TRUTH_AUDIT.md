## Reconciliation policy (PR-06)

Winner selection for each Type-1 conflict, applied by editing **JSON only** (no
parser or write-path change):

1. **Parser (A) name when A participates** — the parser is the canonical Atlas
   surfaces/logs today, so it is the strongest history signal (frozen; PR-14 will
   regenerate parser aliases from the catalog).
2. **Enrichment (D) name when D participates and A does not** — D encodes Dale's
   shorthand→lift_code history (frozen; PR-15 syncs it from the catalog).
3. **Otherwise the coarse, unqualified movement name** — Dale logs the movement,
   not the apparatus, so equipment/position qualifiers (Barbell/Dumbbell/Cable/
   Machine/Standing/Bodyweight/One-Arm/Lying) are dropped from the shared canonical.

Edits: catalog (B) granular entries renamed to the coarse winner; coaching (C)
coarse entries had variant-specific aliases removed where the catalog keeps a
distinct exercise. The owner-reserved Bicep-Curl↔Dumbbell-Curl distinction was NOT
folded (`services/exerciseEnrichment.js`); the mis-alias `bicep curl → Dumbbell
Curl` was removed from the catalog instead.
