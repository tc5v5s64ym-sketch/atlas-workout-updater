`05-coach-communication-intelligence` supports **coach communication policy, coach-note tone, autonomy-supportive language, praise/sandbagging/setback handling, when Atlas should speak vs stay quiet, and conversation-first coaching behavior** — the primary source behind the Communication domain (H) and the Conversation Contract (`docs/CONVERSATION_CONTRACT_V1.md`).

`06-decade-relationship-coaching` (markdown-native, not a PDF) is **long-horizon research guidance only**: what a decade-long coaching relationship requires — relationship memory (decision vs personality stores), coach promises and receipts, trust creation/destruction, communication compression with tenure, return-after-layoff behavior, decision-outcome learning, and the distilled **Atlas Coaching Principles**. It creates no roadmap or implementation items. **Consult it before designing** coach memory, relationship intelligence, proactivity, coach promises, injury memory, return-after-layoff behavior, decision-outcome learning, personalization, long-term programming, or coach communication changes (its Part V has the full checklist).

`07-the-feel-of-atlas` (markdown-native, not a PDF) is **interaction-philosophy research guidance only**: why conversational AI feels effortless (grounding, interactive alignment, turn-taking), the inferred guided↔freestyle coaching spectrum, the conversational intent model (visible vs hidden intent), the rhythm of coaching (Wooden observation data + feedback research), the 16-state athlete conversation-state model, complete workout conversation models, failure modes that destroy coaching trust (sycophancy, nagging, abandonment research), the day-1→year-10 relationship arc, and the **100 interaction principles**. It creates no roadmap or implementation items. **Consult it before designing, changing, or reviewing** anything the athlete experiences in conversation — coach voice, verbosity, proactivity, silence rules, intent handling, clarification behavior, celebration, challenge, error recovery, onboarding feel. Companion to source 06 (the decade's memory/trust architecture); 07 is how each minute of that decade should feel.
